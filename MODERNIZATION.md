# Implementation decisions

The 40-episode order follows the agreed curriculum. Each project preserves its learning concept while replacing old demo UI and fragile handlers with a new teaching implementation. Original archive files remain outside this distribution and unchanged.

The app direction is the design skill’s Framed sheet, seed 672195162: green-grey frame, white working surface, quiet controls and teal on selected data. Portfolio composition uses Split stage, seed 1293059299. For small one-job lessons the large sidebar, marketing image/motion floors and extra sections were omitted to keep the concept understandable. IBM Plex Sans is locally bundled. All product/landscape illustrations are original SVGs; they are not presented as photographs. Sample prices, weather and recipe quantities are clearly identified.

The old Tailwind/CDN recipe becomes semantic HTML with native details and CSS Grid; jQuery custom select becomes native select; hover-only controls gain focus/click alternatives. GSAP/ScrollTrigger remain in 32–33, threejs-toys/Three.js in 35, and Swiper in 40. Local, pinned vendor files retain notices. No-library slider concepts precede those integrations.

Episode 35 is explicitly a library integration. Its library has no public dispose method, so Stop releases its resources by reloading the lesson. Reduced motion and coarse-pointer devices stay static. Canvas scratch has an equivalent reveal button. Audio is locally synthesized. The weather lesson uses Open-Meteo with timeout, cancellation and labelled offline sample; commercial API use needs the appropriate plan.

Episode 01 is the code-package version: the older standalone video artifact is preserved separately. Its unverified archive laptop photo is replaced here with a new vector illustration. No new videos were made for episodes 02–40.
